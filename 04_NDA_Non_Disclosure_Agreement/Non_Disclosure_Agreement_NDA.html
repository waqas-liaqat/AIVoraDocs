1. Purpose of Disclosure

The Parties wish to explore and engage in commercial collaboration involving Artificial Intelligence development, workflow automation, autonomous voice/chat agents, and system integration. During this engagement, each Party may disclose sensitive and proprietary technical and business data.

2. Scope of Confidential Information

Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

  • Client Assets: Customer lists, sales databases, CRM data, proprietary operational procedures, and financial metrics.
  • Agency Assets: Proprietary AI prompt architectures, guardrail logic, n8n/Make automation workflow blueprints, and custom scripts.
  • Security Credentials: API keys (OpenAI, VAPI, Make, Twilio, Stripe), webhook signing secrets, database credentials, and OAuth tokens.

3. Protection Standards & AI Privacy Guarantee

The Receiving Party agrees to exercise reasonable care (at least equal to that used for its own confidential assets) to prevent unauthorized disclosure.

🔒 AI Training & Privacy Safeguard: Aivora Automations explicitly covenants that Client customer data and conversational records shall never be used to train public AI models or distributed to unauthorized third parties.

4. Term & Data Decommissioning

This Agreement remains in force for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date. Upon project termination or written request, all staging API keys, temporary credentials, and client data copies shall be destroyed or returned within five (5) business days.
