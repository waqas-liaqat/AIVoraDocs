## 1. PURPOSE OF DISCLOSURE

The Parties wish to explore and engage in a business relationship involving the consultation, development, deployment, and integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) software, voice agents, chatbots, and workflow automation systems (the "**Authorized Purpose**"). In the course of this engagement, it will be necessary for each Party to disclose certain confidential, proprietary, and technical information to the other.

---

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

2.1 "**Confidential Information**" refers to any and all proprietary, technical, operational, or business data disclosed by one Party to the other, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or through system access, including but not limited to:
- **Client Assets**: Customer databases, CRM records, lead lists, financial metrics, trade secrets, sales processes, internal SOPs, and proprietary business logic.
- **Agency Assets**: Custom AI prompt architectures, guardrail frameworks, proprietary n8n/Make automation workflow templates, code scripts, developer tools, and pricing formulas.
- **Security Credentials**: API keys (e.g., OpenAI, VAPI, Twilio, Make, Stripe), database passwords, webhook signing secrets, OAuth tokens, and server access keys.

2.2 **Exclusions**: Confidential Information does not include information that:
- Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party.
- Was already in the rightful possession of the Receiving Party prior to disclosure.
- Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance upon the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information.
- Is rightfully received from a third party without duty of confidentiality.

---

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

3.1 **Standard of Care**: The Receiving Party agrees to protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of like nature, but in no case less than a reasonable standard of care.

3.2 **Restricted Use**: The Receiving Party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the Authorized Purpose and shall not disclose, reproduce, or distribute it to any third party without prior written consent.

3.3 **AI Training Safeguard**: Aivora Automations expressly agrees that Client data and proprietary customer conversations **shall never be used to train public Large Language Models (LLMs)** or shared with unauthorized third-party platforms.

3.4 **Credential Protection**: All sensitive API keys and login credentials provided during the project shall be stored in encrypted password vaults (e.g., Bitwarden / 1Password / encrypted environment variables) and accessible only to authorized engineers directly assigned to the build.
