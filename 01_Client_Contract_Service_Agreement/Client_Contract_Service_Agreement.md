## 1. PURPOSE & ENGAGEMENT

1.1 **Engagement**: Client hereby engages Aivora Automations, and Aivora Automations agrees, to provide custom Artificial Intelligence (AI) development, workflow automation, chatbot implementation, voice agent deployment, CRM integration, and related consulting services as outlined in applicable **Statements of Work (SOW)** executed pursuant to this Agreement.

1.2 **Structure**: Specific deliverables, timelines, technical specifications, and fee schedules shall be detailed in individual SOWs. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and an SOW, the terms of the SOW shall prevail for that specific project.

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES & DELIVERABLES

2.1 **Core Services Provided**:
- **AI Agents Development**: Custom autonomous agents leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs such as OpenAI GPT-4o, Anthropic Claude, etc.) for multi-step workflow execution.
- **AI Chatbots & Virtual Assistants**: Conversational bots for WhatsApp, web interfaces, and CRMs featuring lead qualification, customer support, and calendar booking.
- **AI Voice Agents**: Inbound and outbound voice calling systems powered by VAPI and telecommunication providers.
- **Workflow & RPA Automation**: Multi-app pipeline integrations utilizing n8n, Make.com, Zapier, custom REST APIs, and webhooks.
- **CRM Automation & Enrichment**: Integration with platforms including HubSpot, GoHighLevel (GHL), Salesforce, Zoho CRM, and Airtable.

2.2 **Out-of-Scope Work**: Any request outside the clearly defined scope in the applicable SOW (including major architecture shifts, additional platform integrations, or unplanned third-party customizations) will require a formal **Change Order** with revised pricing and timelines before work begins.
