1. Purpose & Scope of Agreement

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") governs the terms under which Aivora Automations ("Provider") provides Artificial Intelligence (AI) engineering, workflow automation, autonomous voice/chat agents, and integration services to the Client as outlined in mutually executed Statements of Work ("SOW").

Individual project specifications, specific timelines, detailed deliverables, and payment schedules shall be defined in corresponding SOWs attached hereto.

2. Core Services & Capabilities

Provider designs, builds, and deploys production-grade AI solutions including but not limited to:

  • AI Agents Development: Autonomous multi-step agents leveraging frontier LLMs (OpenAI GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet) for complex decision-making.
  • AI Chatbots & Virtual Assistants: Conversational bots for WhatsApp, web interfaces, and CRMs featuring 24/7 lead qualification and calendar bookings.
  • AI Voice Agents: VAPI-powered human-like inbound/outbound voice systems for appointment scheduling and customer support.
  • Workflow Automation & RPA: Custom multi-platform pipelines utilizing n8n, Make.com, Zapier, Webhooks, and custom APIs.
  • CRM Integration: Deep automation across HubSpot, GoHighLevel (GHL), Salesforce, Zoho CRM, Airtable, and Google Workspace.

3. Project Timelines & Delivery Commitments

Aivora Automations operates with a rapid deployment methodology. Standard project delivery timelines are typically 5 to 14 business days upon receipt of the upfront milestone deposit and required client system access credentials.

Client Dependency Notice: Delivery timelines are reliant upon timely client input. Client agrees to provide required API keys, feedback, and access within two (2) business days of formal request.

4. Payment Terms, Global Invoicing & Taxes

Milestone Trigger / Timing Standard Percentage
Milestone 1: Deposit Upon contract execution & prior to project kickoff 50% of total project fee
Milestone 2: Final Handover Upon successful UAT, testing & final system deployment 50% of total project fee

Invoices are payable within seven (7) calendar days of issuance via International Bank Wire (SWIFT/IBAN), Stripe Checkout, Wise Business, Payoneer, or Elevate Pay. Billed in USD (or mutually agreed foreign currency).

Cross-Border IT Export Tax Notice: Invoices represent cross-border software export services and are billed with 0% local sales tax (Reverse Charge / Export Zero-Rated). Client is responsible for any domestic bank transfer fees in their own jurisdiction.

5. Acceptance Testing & 30-Day Bug-Fix Warranty

Client shall have five (5) business days following deployment to conduct User Acceptance Testing (UAT). Aivora Automations provides an inclusive 30-Day Post-Launch Bug-Fix Warranty to guarantee all workflows function in accordance with agreed technical specifications.

Note: The warranty covers bugs in the original build and does not cover third-party API downtime or unauthorized workflow alterations made by external parties.

6. Intellectual Property & Ownership

Upon receipt of 100% full payment, the Client receives full ownership and intellectual property rights to the specific custom workflows, scripts, prompts, and documentation created under the SOW. Provider retains ownership of its proprietary pre-existing templates and core reusable developer frameworks.

7. Confidentiality, Data Privacy & AI Disclaimer

Both parties agree to treat all business strategies, client records, and API credentials as strictly Confidential Information under international data protection best practices.

AI Training Safeguard: Client customer data and conversational records shall never be used to train public LLM models.

AI Probabilistic Output Disclaimer: Client acknowledges that modern AI models generate probabilistic responses. While Provider engineers rigorous guardrails, system prompts, and validation layers, Provider does not guarantee 100% deterministic outputs from external foundational LLM providers.

8. Governing Law & International Dispute Resolution

In the event of any dispute, the Parties agree to first seek amicable resolution via senior negotiation within 14 days. If unresolved, disputes shall be settled through international commercial arbitration in English under standard ICC/SIAC rules or under the laws of a mutually agreed neutral jurisdiction.
