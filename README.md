## ⚡ How to Edit & Print Documents Live (No Coding Needed)

Every document template has a built-in **Live Editor** right in your browser:

1. Open **`index.html`** in Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Safari.
2. Click **"✏️ Open & Edit Live"** on any document card.
3. **Type & Customize**: Click directly on any text, client name, price, date, line item, or bullet point and type your changes.
4. **Auto-Save Draft**: Click **"💾 Save Draft"** to preserve your edits in your browser's local memory.
5. **Generate Client PDF**: Click **"🖨️ Print / Save as PDF"** (or press `Ctrl + P` / `Cmd + P`):
   - Select **"Save as PDF"** as the printer destination.
   - Check **"Background graphics"** (to print beautiful gradients & badges).
   - Click **Save** — you get a clean, executive-grade branded PDF with zero edit borders or buttons!
